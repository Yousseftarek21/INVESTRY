FAQs

How do subscriptions work?

Pro is billed monthly or annually through Stripe, on this website. Your subscription applies to your account and unlocks Pro instantly in the app too.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from this page — no calls, no forms, no waiting.

Will my data stay safe?

Yes. Your portfolio data is yours whether you're on Free or Pro, and payments are handled entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes, anytime from the billing portal — your next invoice is prorated automatically.

What happens if my subscription expires?

You're moved back to the Free plan automatically. Nothing you've already entered is ever deleted.
